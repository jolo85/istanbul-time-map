What this is

A map of Istanbul with your own location on it, showing the buildings that stood here at a moment in the past.

  • Pick a year along the bottom. The pins change to what existed then.
  • Press ◎ to put yourself on the map.
  • Press ☰ for a list of what is nearest to you, closest first.
  • Tap any pin for what it was and when.

Pin positions come from Wikidata, wall and ruin outlines from OpenStreetMap. For buildings that are gone the position is the best known spot, not a survey. There is no real map of the city from before about 1800, so the old periods show buildings rather than streets.
